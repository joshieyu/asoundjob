# Audio System Engineer

## Description
At Bose Corporation, we believe sound is the most powerful force on earth — and for over 60 years, we have been a company built on innovation, excellence, and independence. Privately owned, fiercely customer-focused, and driven by our values, we continue to lead industries and transform lives through sound. 

Today, Bose Corporation is entering an exciting new era. Across multiple global Business Units and Global Functions, we are shaping the future of audio technology, automotive, luxury, and premium experiences. We invite you to join us in this transformation. 

## Job Description
Performs Engineering work in operations, production, construction or maintenance environments. Responsibilities are within the Engineering Function as a generalist or in a combination of Disciplines.

### Key Responsibilities
Acoustic engineer supports audio system design, conducts audio performance evaluation and tuning of vehicle audio systems to achieve Bose premium sound. He or she will be capable of helping us to move toward more efficient and automated tuning.

* Support audio system design at concept stage.
* Conduct audio system performance evaluation at concept stage. Complete concept car audio tuning and get both internal and customer agreement.
* Support speaker layout/package activities during development.
* Measure and analyze acoustical and electrical system performance.
* Conduct audio tuning of prototype vehicles at customer site and get agreement of both internal to Bose and customer.
* Documentation/reporting to internal and customer.
* Conduct audits of production vehicles to make sure the acoustic performance are reproduced as intended.
* Conduct component level acoustic evaluation as needed.
* Submit required documents in related to activities above both internal and to customer.
* Attend design review to confirm audio performance as needed.
* Support our technology event – mainly conduct technical demonstration.
* In vehicle and bench testing of amplifier function verification.

### Knowledge and Skill Requirements
* **Customer orientation**: Demonstrates a commitment to understand, meet, and exceed the needs of those who can benefit from Bose products and services.
* **Team interaction**: Enjoys working with other team members in collaboration, learning and training. Understands working together can bring positive results, and thrives as part of a larger team.
* **Communication**: Clearly conveys information and ideas through a variety of media to individuals or groups in a manner that engages the audience and helps them understand and retain the message. Fluent in oral and writing in English is preferred.
* **Passion**: Demonstrates intensity, excitement, and enthusiasm for the work at hand.
* **Technical knowledge**: Has achieved a satisfactory level of technical and professional skill or knowledge in position-related areas; keeps abreast of current developments and trends in areas of expertise.
* **Develops self**: Actively identifies new areas for learning; regularly creates and takes advantage of learning opportunities; uses newly gained knowledge and skill on the job and learns through their application.

### Qualification, Education and Experiences
* Bachelor’s or master degree of electrical engineering, acoustic engineering or physics required.
* Experience or knowledge in the acoustic design, analysis and measurement required.
* Experience in DSP application required.
* Experience in automotive engineering preferred.
* Experience in software design, development preferred.
