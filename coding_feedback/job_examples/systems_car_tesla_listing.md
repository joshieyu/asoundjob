# Audio Hardware Engineer, Speakers

## What to Expect
Tesla is seeking an Audio/Acoustic Engineer within the Infotainment Team who has had 5+ years of design, test and release responsibility of Acoustic, Loudspeaker and Signal Processing products. This Engineer should have a strong understanding of the application & design of loudspeakers and loudspeaker systems, and the ability to define, specify and set the requirements for these automotive acoustic products. This engineer will also be a technical and process mentor for other engineers in the team and the company. This Engineer must thrive in a start-up company environment with great opportunities for growth and spectacular achievement.

## What You’ll Do
- Product development of loudspeaker transducer products from inception to end of life
- Knowledge of our audio systems, relative vehicle environment and integration
- Work with cross functional teams to create the best possible integration of the product
- Interpret, develop and manage product drawings and specifications
- Work with suppliers on design, development, and validation of audio products
- Testing of audio systems and components
- Plan, execute, interpret, and document product validation
- Component and system level troubleshooting

## What You’ll Bring
- Degree in Engineering with minimum 5 years of experience with developing audio products from scratch or equivalent experience
- Experience in audio products (acoustics and electronics)
- Experience working with cross functional team-members from multiple disciplines to reach fact-based decision
- Technical and Product Management skills to communicate and drive suppliers
- Strong problem-solving skills and desire to work in fast paced environment
- Hands-on experience designing and debugging products
- Previous CAD exposure
- Experience with environmental constraints (temp, humidity, and vibration), regulatory compliance and product debug (EMC/EMI, ESD, transients, etc.)
- Experience in analog and digital circuit analysis
