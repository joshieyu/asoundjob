# Job Title: Audio Systems Engineer

**Company:** Meta  
**Location:** Sunnyvale, CA (+1 location)  
**Department:** Engineering  
**Compensation:** $144,000/year to $204,000/year + bonus + equity + benefits  
**Source URL:** https://www.metacareers.com/profile/job_details/1587760759663502/  

## Overview
Meta's Hardware Audio Team is hiring an Audio Systems Engineer to help develop, ship, and optimize high-quality audio systems and experiences for next-generation wearables at the intersection of hardware, software, and content. The role focuses on end-to-end product audio development—from proof-of-concept prototyping through production readiness—including analyzing/defining, tuning, and debugging real-time audio capture systems, as well as designing, developing, testing, and optimizing product-level audio technologies. The ideal candidate brings strong background in audio system design, acoustic simulation, and digital signal processing (DSP), and will partner closely with cross-functional teams to drive innovation and deliver top-tier audio performance.

## Responsibilities
- Design, develop, and test audio systems and technologies for wearables products.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to drive innovative solutions and champion best practices for audio system design.
- Analyze, define, tune, and debug real-time audio capture systems for forward-looking products and user experiences.
- Develop and manage operations in audio labs, including standardized verification and novel experiments.
- Conduct HW and SW testing on consumer electronics, product benchmarking, and data collection for algorithm tuning and development.
- Support product and audio technology demo setup.

## Minimum Qualifications
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Acoustics, Electrical Engineering, Computer Science, Digital Signal Processing or a related field, and/or equivalent industry experience.
- 6+ years of experience in audio system design, acoustic simulation, and digital signal processing.
- Experience with audio hardware testing and related tools, such as HATS, precision microphones, anechoic chambers.
- Proficiency in programming languages such as MATLAB, Python, and/or MaxMSP.
- Effective communication, documentation, and presentation skills.

## Preferred Qualifications
- Experience with psycho-acoustic metrics for speech quality and intelligibility.
- Experience independently driving audio workstreams in a dynamic cross-functional environment, from product concept to mass production.
- Familiarity with human perception of spatial sound.
- Familiarity with typical DSP building blocks such as digital biquad filters, IIR, FIR, AGC, wide-band/multi-band DRC, and audio capture algorithms such as beamforming, echo cancellation, and noise suppression.
- Experience in acoustics and consumer audio EE architecture.
- Familiarity with acoustic simulation software, e.g. COMSOL.
- Familiarity with audio algorithm development and/or machine learning techniques.
- 4+ years of experience designing and deploying objective test methodologies for consumer electronics devices and audio algorithms or features such as voice communication, voice assistant, video capture.
- 4+ years of experience defining, tuning, and testing efficient microphone-array processing and real-time audio capture algorithms.
