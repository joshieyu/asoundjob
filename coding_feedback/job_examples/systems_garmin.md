# Senior Audio Engineer

## Overview
We are seeking a full-time **Senior Audio Engineer** in Miramar, FL. In this role, you will be responsible for providing advanced acoustic engineering solutions for audio systems, products, and equipment.

## Essential Functions
- Complete complex acoustic designs for Garmin or OEM products independently with general guidance from others to accomplish technical and project goals
- Leads projects, provides guidance, and mentors less experienced audio engineers
- Design core acoustic components and systems
- Perform acoustic simulations to achieve best performance with the most efficient designs
- Select components and perform product development and release activities to qualify and place new products into mass production
- Create Bill of Materials (BOMs), testing documents, qualification/verification procedures related to audio product manufacturing
- Simulate acoustic components and assemblies; judge results and modify accordingly
- Prototype and test audio products using specialized audio equipment
- Perform audio tuning using in-house and external tools
- Design and/or support in-house development applications and/or equipment used for audio optimization, product qualification, etc.
- Work with a cross functional team of hardware and firmware engineers allowing test and debug of complete audio systems
- Provide technical assistance and support to customer support and technical training teams where needed
- Maintain configuration control of documentation for products in production and interface with available drafting and document control resources
- Apply design standards and procedures to implement complex designs
- Identify and resolve defects of complex scope using proper engineering tools and techniques

## Basic Qualifications
- Bachelor's Degree in Engineering or a related technology field **AND** a minimum of 5 years of relevant audio engineering experience **OR** an equivalent combination of education and experience
- Excellent academics (cumulative GPA greater than or equal to 3.0 as a general rule) with the demonstrated ability to apply learned knowledge
- Demonstrated strong and effective verbal, written, and interpersonal communication skills

## Desired Qualifications
- Previous experience working in a team environment
- Previous work or internship experience in design, particularly for consumer products
- Outstanding academics (cumulative GPA greater than or equal to 3.5)
- Audio systems repair or troubleshooting experience
