# Engineer II, Audio Electronics Design

**Job ID:** 2026-4862
**Category:** Engineering
**Locations:** US-IL-Niles
**Workplace Arrangement:** Hybrid, On-site (4 days a week)

## Overview
*Design the future of audio!*

As an *Engineer II, Audio Electronics Design*, you will contribute to the design, development, verification, and support of electronic circuits and printed circuit board assemblies for high-quality professional audio products.

In this role, you will work on analog, digital, power, mixed-signal, and embedded system hardware used in products such as wired microphones, wireless microphones, signal processors, conference systems, and related accessories. You will support the full product-development process, including circuit design, simulation, prototyping, hardware verification testing, troubleshooting, documentation, and cross-functional collaboration.

The ideal candidate can apply engineering judgment within defined procedures and practices, communicate clearly with project teams, and contribute hands-on technical work in a collaborative product-development environment.

*This role is based in our Niles, IL headquarters and will be hybrid, on-site 4 days a week.*

## Responsibilities
* Design, simulate, prototype, test, and troubleshoot analog, digital, power, and mixed-signal circuitry for professional audio products.
* Develop and support printed circuit board assemblies, including schematic design, PCB layout, PCBA bring-up, circuit modification, and design verification.
* Perform circuit verification, system/subsystem testing, power verification, signal integrity checks, audio performance testing, and functional testing.
* Analyze and resolve circuit defects, performance issues, compatibility issues, and hardware problems discovered during development, production, or in the field.
* Create and maintain technical documentation, including design notes, test procedures, verification results, design specifications, issue summaries, and supporting product-development documentation.
* Support cross-functional product-development activities with hardware, embedded/software, mechanical, acoustics, automated test, system verification, manufacturing, quality, sourcing, and compliance.
* Support manufacturing test, automated test development, board programming, test procedure development, and production-readiness activities as needed.

## Qualifications
* Bachelor’s degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, or a related field and a minimum of 2 years of related experience; or an advanced degree without experience.
* Experience with circuit design, simulation, prototyping, testing, troubleshooting, defect analysis, and technical documentation.
* Working knowledge of analog circuitry, including op-amps, BJTs, FETs, LDOs, switching supplies, and related analog or power components.
* Working knowledge of digital circuitry, including digital logic, embedded microcontrollers, networking, ADCs, DACs, DSPs, FPGAs, and related digital components.
* Knowledge of audio-related circuits, audio systems, and audio measurement techniques.
* Experience using lab equipment such as oscilloscopes, power supplies, multimeters, and other electrical or audio measurement tools.
* Experience with schematic CAD tools, PCB layout processes, and SPICE or other circuit simulation tools.
* Hands-on soldering, circuit modification, prototype assembly, or circuit repair skills.
* Strong written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to document work activities, collaborate with peers and project staff, and incorporate feedback from other team members.
* **Preferred:** experience with programming or test scripting for hardware verification, automation, data collection, or analysis.
* **Preferred:** experience with embedded systems, Linux, Python, RTOS concepts, hardware/software co-design, manufacturing test support, or test engineering support.
